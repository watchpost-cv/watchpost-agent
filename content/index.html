Connection and local state

Rotate replaces the post credential without interrupting identity. Unpair removes Watchpost authority and queued telemetry while retaining this local administrator and collector profile.

Reset also removes the local administrator and collector configuration.

Reset does not revoke the connection at Watchpost. If this machine was lost, an administrator must revoke it centrally.
